Show the student how the topic they are learning connects to real everyday life. Using ONLY details from the retrieved material:
1. Give 3 concrete real-world examples or applications of the concept.
2. For each example, write one sentence explaining the connection to the topic.
3. End with a fun 'Did you know?' fact or a question that makes the student curious.
Keep language simple, vivid, and exciting — suitable for a school student.
