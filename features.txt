────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  PART 1 — THE BIG PICTURE: WHY EEC IS DIFFERENT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Most school management systems are digital filing cabinets — they store data but
do not act on it. EEC is the first platform that combines school operations,
predictive Machine Learning, and a school-specific RAG AI Tutor into a single
multi-tenant product. Every AI response, every insight, every recommendation is
derived exclusively from that school's own approved educational content — never
from generic internet sources.

This means:
  • A student can learn ALONE, at any TIME, from ANYWHERE.
  • A teacher's routine busywork is automated, freeing them for research,
    mentorship, and higher-order instruction.
  • A principal gets predictive dashboards instead of lagging reports.
  • A parent is a real-time partner in their child's education, not a term-end
    audience.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  PART 2 — STUDENT-FACING AI & LEARNING FEATURES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

2.1  AI TUTOR — ALWAYS ON, ALWAYS SCHOOL-SPECIFIC
  ▸ Powered by RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) over the school's own
    teaching materials — textbooks, notes, slides, PDFs.
  ▸ Students get accurate, curriculum-aligned answers 24 / 7, with zero
    risk of off-topic or internet-sourced misinformation.
  ▸ Six interaction modes in a single chat interface:
      → EXPLAIN    — clear, layered concept explanations at student level
      → QUIZ       — auto-generated MCQ quizzes with instant feedback
      → FLASHCARDS — 3D flip-card study sets with "Got it / Still learning" tracking
      → NOTES      — structured, colour-coded notes from any topic
      → MIND MAP   — visual concept maps with animated branch connections
      → HOMEWORK HELP — Socratic guidance (never gives the answer directly;
                         leads students to discover it themselves)

2.2  SOCRATIC HOMEWORK HELP (UNIQUE TO EEC)
  ▸ The AI will NEVER just hand over the answer.
  ▸ It follows 7 embedded Socratic rules: ask guiding questions, give clues
    on "I don't know", confirm only after the student states the answer.
  ▸ Builds genuine understanding, not answer-copying behaviour.
  ▸ Differentiates EEC from any chatbot that simply resolves homework instantly.

2.3  ADAPTIVE LEARNING PATH ENGINE
  ▸ ML-generated personalised learning paths per student based on mastery
    scores, error patterns, and pace data.
  ▸ Spaced Repetition scheduling ensures concepts are reviewed at optimal
    intervals — automatically, with no teacher input needed.
  ▸ Wrong-answer review sessions isolate exactly where a student went wrong
    and serve targeted practice on those gaps.

2.4  MASTERY & ENGAGEMENT INTELLIGENCE
  ▸ Mastery score per subject/chapter computed from exam results, practice
    attempts, AI quiz performance, and review streaks.
  ▸ Engagement analytics track time-on-task, AI tutor sessions, and forum
    activity — surfaced to students as progress gamification.
  ▸ At-risk flags triggered before a student fails, not after.

2.5  BASELINE DIAGNOSTIC + LEARNING GAP ANALYSIS
  ▸ On enrolment or at the start of a term, a baseline quiz identifies
    existing knowledge gaps.
  ▸ The platform constructs an individual gap profile and adjusts
    recommended content and AI tutor focus accordingly.

2.6  MOCK EXAMS, PRACTICE PAPERS & AI QUESTION GENERATION
  ▸ Teachers upload a question bank; ML selects a balanced, curriculum-aligned
    paper with the right difficulty distribution.
  ▸ Students get instant AI-graded feedback with explanations for every wrong
    answer.
  ▸ Tryout / practice-paper engine lets students self-test anytime without
    teacher scheduling.

2.7  GAMIFICATION & ACADEMIC ALCOVE
  ▸ Achievements, streaks, and leaderboards reward consistent study.
  ▸ Dashboard Pet — a live companion that evolves as the student progresses.
  ▸ Alcove: a moderated academic discussion forum for peer learning.
  ▸ Learning Path Map with visual milestones makes progress tangible.

2.8  STUDENT WELLBEING TRACKING
  ▸ Integrated wellbeing check-ins surface emotional and social signals.
  ▸ Teachers and counsellors are notified of at-risk wellbeing patterns —
    the platform bridges academic and pastoral care.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  PART 3 — TEACHER WORKLOAD REDUCTION & PROFESSIONAL EMPOWERMENT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The average teacher spends 40–60% of their working hours on administrative tasks
that do not directly improve student outcomes. EEC automates that layer entirely.

3.1  AI-ASSISTED LESSON PLAN CREATION
  ▸ Teachers describe learning objectives; AI drafts a structured lesson plan
    aligned to the curriculum map.
  ▸ Lesson plan status tracked across classes — no more manual follow-up.
  ▸ Teachers review, approve, and publish — reducing plan-creation time by
    an estimated 70%.

3.2  AI QUESTION BANK GENERATION
  ▸ Teachers upload a topic or chapter; the AI service generates MCQ,
    short-answer, and long-answer questions at configurable difficulty levels.
  ▸ Teachers review and curate — they never start from a blank page.

3.3  AUTOMATED ATTENDANCE MANAGEMENT
  ▸ One-click digital attendance with automatic absence notifications to parents.
  ▸ Attendance analytics per class, subject, and student — surfaced without
    any manual report generation.

3.4  TIMETABLE & ALLOCATION AUTOMATION
  ▸ Conflict-free timetable generation with teacher allocation management.
  ▸ Substitution scheduling handled in the platform — no WhatsApp chains.

3.5  EXAM & RESULT WORKFLOW
  ▸ End-to-end exam lifecycle: question paper → conduct → grading → result
    publication → report cards.
  ▸ AI-assisted grading for objective questions; structured grading tools
    for subjective answers.
  ▸ Report cards auto-generated from result data.

3.6  TEACHER ANALYTICS DASHBOARD
  ▸ Class-level mastery heatmaps show exactly which topics need re-teaching.
  ▸ Per-student engagement and risk scores let teachers focus interventions
    where they matter most — not spread attention uniformly.
  ▸ Teachers freed from data-gathering can invest time in differentiated
    instruction, collaborative research, and professional development.

3.7  BEHAVIOUR & INTERVENTION RECORDS
  ▸ Digital log of observations, interventions, and outcomes per student.
  ▸ Pattern detection alerts teachers when behaviour trends require escalation.

3.8  LEAVE, EXPENSES & STAFF SELF-SERVICE
  ▸ Teachers manage their own leave requests, expense claims, and schedules
    inside the platform — no parallel HR tools needed.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  PART 4 — MACHINE LEARNING ENGINE (EEC'S CORE MOAT)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1  PREDICTIVE AT-RISK DETECTION
  ▸ ML models trained on attendance patterns, assessment trends, engagement
    signals, and wellbeing data.
  ▸ Flags at-risk students weeks before failure — actionable, not retrospective.

4.2  MASTERY SCORING
  ▸ Continuous mastery computation per student per topic based on spaced
    repetition performance, exam scores, and AI quiz results.
  ▸ Not a static grade — a live learning signal updated with every interaction.

4.3  ENGAGEMENT SCORING
  ▸ Multi-signal engagement index: session frequency, AI tutor usage, forum
    participation, homework submission rate.
  ▸ Identifies disengaged students who still pass tests — the hidden risk group.

4.4  LEARNING PACE MODELLING
  ▸ Each student's pace profile determines how fast new content is introduced
    and how aggressively spaced repetition recurs.
  ▸ Slower learners get more review cycles; faster learners get enrichment.

4.5  TREND ANALYSIS & FORECASTING
  ▸ Class-level and school-level trend dashboards for principals and admins.
  ▸ Forecast end-of-term outcomes based on mid-term data — enabling proactive
    academic interventions.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  PART 6 — ADMINISTRATION & MULTI-TENANCY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.1  ZERO-SETUP MULTI-TENANCY
  ▸ Every school gets a fully isolated environment: data, branding, payment
    gateway, curriculum, and AI knowledge base are completely separate.
  ▸ No cross-school data leakage by design — school_id isolation enforced at
    every query, AI retrieval filter, and API boundary.

6.2  ORGANISATION-SPECIFIC AI KNOWLEDGE BASE
  ▸ The RAG AI Tutor ingests ONLY that school's uploaded materials.
  ▸ Students cannot access another school's content — curriculum integrity
    is guaranteed at the vector-search level.

6.3  INTEGRATED FEE & SUBSCRIPTION BILLING
  ▸ Schools run their own Razorpay accounts for student fee collection.
  ▸ EEC subscription billing is tiered by active student count — schools
    pay proportionally as they grow.
  ▸ AES-256-GCM encryption for all stored payment credentials.

6.4  AUDIT LOGS & COMPLIANCE
  ▸ Full audit trail for user actions, payment events, data changes, and
    security events.
  ▸ Structured Pino logging with correlation IDs for every request —
    production-grade observability built in.

6.5  SUPER ADMIN MONITORING
  ▸ Platform-wide organisation health, subscription status, billing tiers,
    and compliance dashboards from a single super-admin portal.
  ▸ Announcement broadcasting and organisation lifecycle management.
