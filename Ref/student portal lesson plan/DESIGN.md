## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an inviting, distraction-free educational sanctuary tailored specifically for 5th-grade learners (ages 10–11). At this developmental milestone, students thrive when complex mathematical concepts feel like an adventurous quest rather than an intimidating administrative chore.

The visual style blends **Soft Tactile Playfulness** with **Modern Clean Ergonomics**. Administrative density, rigid enterprise grids, and overwhelming tables are replaced with generous soft shapes, warm illuminated tones, and gamified pacing cues. Every interface component reinforces feelings of confidence, safety, curiosity, and accomplishment. 

Key attributes:
- **Gamified Quest Mechanics:** Clear progression paths, milestone markers, energetic step nodes, and celebratory micro-states.
- **Cognitive Serenity:** High whitespace cushions, collapsed background administrative tasks, and an uncompromising focus on a single primary call to action: the student's immediate next step.
- **Friendly Tangibility:** Tactile pills, soft 3D-inspired drop depths, and organic curvature (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl`) that reduce cognitive tension.

## Colors

The palette balances joyful visual energy with accessibility and clarity:

- **Primary (`#635BFF` — Radiant Indigo):** The anchor for active quests, focal buttons, active step indicators, and primary branding. It projects optimism, discovery, and clarity.
- **Secondary (`#FF8A48` — Warm Coral/Amber):** Accentuates incentives, curiosity hooks, motivational highlights, badges, and warm discovery states.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` — Fresh Mint Emerald):** Signals progression, completed steps, mastery achievements, and successful validations.
- **Neutral (`#475569` — Slate):** Delivers accessible, soft-yet-legible body text without the harshness of pure black.

### Canvas & Background Strategy
- **Base Canvas:** `#F8FAFC` to `#F4F5FB` (Warm, comforting white tinted with soft violet-slate).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure `#FFFFFF` with whisper-soft borders (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Interactive Highlight Backgrounds:** Lavender mist (`#EEEDFE`) for soft badges, hints, and active quest step containers.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** is utilized universally across headlines, body copy, and UI controls. Its rounded apertures, friendly humanist terminals, and robust geometric foundation maximize legibility for young readers while avoiding childish or condescending letterforms.

- **Headlines & Quest Milestones:** Expressive weight (700/800) creates instant visual orientation without intimidating density.
- **Narrative & Explanation Body:** Set at generous line-heights (1.5–1.6) with medium weights (400/500) to assist emerging readers in parsing compound word problems and concept scenarios.
- **Micro-labels and Badges:** Uppercase and tracked letterforms are kept minimal and friendly, reserving prominent weights for immediate numerical stats and status flags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a structured, fluid 12-column grid focused on an **Uncluttered 2-Zone Stage**:
1. **Left Focus Rail (4 columns on Desktop):** Houses the "What You'll Discover" capsule and supporting concept milestones.
2. **Main Quest Stage (8 columns on Desktop):** Hosts the dynamic Lesson Header, Progress Ring, and the sequential Step-by-Step Quest Path.

### Responsive Behavior
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Collapsible slim sidebar for navigation; two-column learning experience with prominent hero card; 24px gutters and 32px canvas margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Side rail collapses beneath the hero teaser into a single column sequence; sidebar converts to a quick bottom-sheet bar; 20px gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single vertical stream; navigation docks into a discreet, child-safe bottom drawer; sticky persistent "Next Step" bar at screen base; 16px margins.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Soft Tactile Layering** with warm, diffused shadows tinted slightly with violet and slate. This avoids dark, clinical drop shadows and creates a playful, approachable physical sensation.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Neutral canvas `#F8FAFC`, zero shadow.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Lesson Containers):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` paired with border `1px solid #E2E8F0` and subtle elevation: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(99, 91, 255, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(71, 85, 105, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Quest Steps & Hover States):** `0 10px 25px -4px rgba(99, 91, 255, 0.12), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(99, 91, 255, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Hero Buttons & Floating Dialogs):** `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(99, 91, 255, 0.25), 0 6px 14px -3px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces generous, pill-forward rounded shapes (`roundedness: 3`). 

- **Primary Cards & Containers:** Standardized at `24px` (`rounded-2xl`) to `32px` (`rounded-3xl`), eliminating sharp angles that evoke rigid test environments.
- **Step Nodes & Badges:** Fully circular (`50%` or `rounded-full`) for quest numbers and completion icons.
- **Interactive Action Buttons:** Pill-shaped capsules (`rounded-full` or `9999px`) to create an inviting, pressable affordance that encourages exploration.

## Components

### 1. Hero Quest Action Button
- **Structure:** Pill-shaped, generous height (`52px` - `56px`), bold text with high-visibility directional icon (`arrow-right` or playful lightning bolt).
- **Styling:** Primary Indigo gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #635BFF 0%, #7E78FF 100%)`) with a tactile bottom inner highlight or subtle press shadow.
- **States:** Hover introduces gentle vertical lift (`-2px`); active state mimics a physical button press (`scale(0.98)`).

### 2. Step-by-Step Journey Map
- **Track Line:** Vertical connecting spine (`4px` width) in soft slate `#E2E8F0` with completed sections filled in Emerald `#10B981`.
- **Step Nodes:**
  - *Completed:* Emerald background, white checkmark icon, glowing subtle emerald halo.
  - *Active / Current:* Large Indigo circle (`44px`), bold numeral, animated pulsing outer aura (`rgba(99, 91, 255, 0.2)`).
  - *Upcoming:* Pale slate neutral node (`#F1F5F9`), slate text (`#94A3B8`), locked state indicated with gentle opacity.
- **Step Card Content:** Encapsulated inside `rounded-2xl` containers. Active card features a soft lavender border highlight (`#635BFF`) and an embedded "Explore Step" action pill.

### 3. Lesson Progress Indicator Ring
- **Format:** Circular radial donut indicator or oversized pill.
- **Styling:** Soft background track with emerald-to-indigo animated stroke.
- **Centerpiece:** Displays current percentage (e.g., "0%") accompanied by an encouraging status phrase ("Ready to begin!", "Great momentum!").

### 4. Curiosity & Narrative Teaser Cards
- **Structure:** Gentle pastel tint (`#F8F9FF` or soft coral glow), rounded-2xl container.
- **Details:** Includes friendly visual icons (rocket, fuel tank, balance scales) that anchor the lesson in real-world story scenarios rather than pure formulaic mathematics.

### 5. Chips & Metadata Pills
- **Styling:** `rounded-full`, low contrast backgrounds (`#F1F5F9` or `#EEEDFE`), paired with vivid category text (`#635BFF` or `#FF8A48`).
- **Function:** Highlight essential context (e.g., "10 min", "The Hook", "I Do") without visual clutter.

### 6. Minimalist Navigation
- **Behavior:** Main administrative links (Grades, Roll number, School ERP sync) are gently tucked away into a single collapsed profile capsule.
- **Header:** Clean breadcrumb path ("Math > Chapter 004") alongside a single reassuring back button ("Back to Chapters").
